# Share an OpenCyber Academy lesson

OpenCyber Academy offers free, public cybersecurity learning material. Start with a specific published lesson, try its exercise, and compare your reasoning with the worked answer. Reading does not require an account.

The [site sharing page](https://opencyber.academy/share/) offers ready-to-copy invitations for readers in English, Portuguese and Spanish.

| Language | Published lesson | Explore more |
|---|---|---|
| English | [Cybersecurity: foundations and decisions](https://opencyber.academy/courses/rapid-1-month/cybersecurity/chapters/en/1/) | [Browse subjects](https://opencyber.academy/subjects/) |
| Português | [Segurança da informação: capítulo 1](https://pt.opencyber.academy/courses/rapid-1-month/information-security/chapters/pt/1/) | [Explorar assuntos](https://pt.opencyber.academy/subjects/) |
| Español | [Seguridad de la información: capítulo 1](https://es.opencyber.academy/courses/rapid-1-month/information-security/chapters/es/1/) | [Explorar temas](https://es.opencyber.academy/subjects/) |

These links point to individual published chapters. Follow each course guide for what is available next in that language; a planned chapter is not a published lesson.

## Short messages you can adapt

**English:** Want a free way to study cybersecurity? OpenCyber Academy has public lessons with worked examples, exercises and answers. Try [this first chapter](https://opencyber.academy/courses/rapid-1-month/cybersecurity/chapters/en/1/) and follow its course guide. No account is needed to read.

**Português:** Quer estudar cibersegurança gratuitamente? A OpenCyber Academy publica aulas abertas com exemplos, exercícios e respostas comentadas. Comece por [este capítulo](https://pt.opencyber.academy/courses/rapid-1-month/information-security/chapters/pt/1/) e siga o guia do curso. A leitura não exige cadastro.

**Español:** ¿Quieres estudiar ciberseguridad gratis? OpenCyber Academy ofrece lecciones abiertas con ejemplos, ejercicios y respuestas explicadas. Empieza por [este capítulo](https://es.opencyber.academy/courses/rapid-1-month/information-security/chapters/es/1/) y sigue la guía del curso. No necesitas registrarte para leer.

For a study group, share the lesson link and ask participants to attempt the exercise before reading the answer. For a class, check the [rights notice](RIGHTS.md) before reusing material or third-party sources. OpenCyber Academy provides self-directed education, not an accredited qualification.
