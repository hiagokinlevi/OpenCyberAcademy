# OpenCyber Academy learning and editorial roadmap

**Authorship and editorial responsibility:** [hiagokinlevi](https://github.com/hiagokinlevi). This is the public learner-facing plan. Construction tools and migration records remain in the private OpenCyber Academy Engine.

## Current curriculum

The [academic map](AREA_MAP.md) has **23 broad areas in five schools**. Every [level](levels/README.md) contains all 23 area courses, each with nine language editions: **207 distinct course designs** and **1,863 planned language editions**. Each course has one principal book per language, with one curricular subject per chapter. English has **4,186 planned subjects** and **207 course skills**; 336 English briefs now have individual teaching outlines and 3,850 remain working summaries. The other language plans await translation. Five English manuscript drafts are public, and **no book is yet complete**. See the [progress review](STRUCTURE_REVIEW.md).

## English production, beginning with Incident Response

Author sequential batches of **five to ten subject-chapters** when that many remain in a course. Before publishing each batch, review transitions, evidence release, technical depth, exercise/answer correspondence and course-skill guidance. The latest batch **deepens technical Incident Response Chapters 1–5 in sequence**, following the first complete draft of Chapters 1–18. Every authored chapter ends with previous/index/next navigation; a link to a plan identifies an unwritten lesson.

1. **Write and validate the books in sequential batches.** The [rapid Incident Response draft](levels/rapid-1-month/incident-response-resilience/en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md) has four authored subjects, 50 exercises and answers, and a 105-page A4 preflight. The [professional Incident Response draft](levels/professional-6-months/incident-response-resilience/en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md) has twelve of twelve draft subjects, EX001–EX060 and matching worked answers; its hash-checked A4 preflight is 63 pages. The [technical Incident Response draft](levels/technical-18-months/incident-response-resilience/en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md) has eighteen of eighteen subject drafts, 72 exercise-answer pairs and a **94-page** A4 preflight. Chapters 1–5 now include additional teaching, bounded worked replays and source-limit checks; the [course skill](levels/technical-18-months/incident-response-resilience/skill/SKILL.md) routes AI tutors through them. All three books need substantial expansion, specialist review and editing before the 300-page release gate. The next writing block deepens technical Chapters **6–10**, followed by later technical chapters, review of the three Incident Response drafts and the technologist course.
2. Refine the English chapter plans alongside book writing. The tailored Incident Response set has 182 briefs; Cybersecurity has 154 of 182. Its remaining 28 advanced briefs and the other 3,822 English briefs are editorial work, but they do not substitute for authored lessons. Resolve the Incident Response specialist-review findings as the first book advances.
3. Continue the [Incident Response area](levels/rapid-1-month/incident-response-resilience/README.md) from the professional draft through technical, technologist, bachelor's, engineering, postgraduate, master's and doctoral research levels. All nine levels now have subject-specific English grids; review their technical depth and dependencies while writing each distinct book.
4. Work through the remaining 22 areas in English, maintaining one distinct book per course. The earlier [Cybersecurity](levels/rapid-1-month/cybersecurity/en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md) and [Security Governance](levels/rapid-1-month/security-governance-risk-compliance/en/book/MANUSCRIPT.md) drafts remain available but do not change the new writing priority.
5. Review and edit the **entire English set** for technical accuracy, progression, source rights, exercises, accessibility and language. Render each book to A4 and verify the [completion gate](BOOK_STANDARD.md) before changing its status to complete.

## Translation order

6. Translate, technically review and edit the full set in **Portuguese** from the approved English editions.
7. Repeat for **Spanish**.
8. **Gate:** confirm 100% verified completion of the English, Portuguese and Spanish collections separately: all 207 complete course editions per language, full weekday curricula, publication review, current translation parity and public site/MCP readback. The [book standard](BOOK_STANDARD.md#sequential-production-order) defines the evidence. Only then begin planned work in **Hebrew, French, Russian, German, Chinese and Japanese**, one language at a time. Preserve existing public material in those languages meanwhile.

The public repository contains plans and drafts during production. A skill, folder or public manuscript does not claim a finished book. Course names describe intended study depth and duration, not formal academic recognition.

The [writing-progress index](WRITING_PROGRESS.md) tracks structural chapter coverage by area. The [course writing table](COURSE_WRITING_PROGRESS.md) tracks all 207 English courses individually, including current manuscript words and measured A4 length where the manuscript hash matches a recorded preflight. The [structure review](STRUCTURE_REVIEW.md) separately tracks completed books and editorial gates; a drafted chapter or page-floor percentage is not a completed book.
